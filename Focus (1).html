How do I export from Google Calendar?

On a computer: calendar.google.com → ⚙ Settings → Import & export → Export. You get a .zip; unzip it (on iPhone, tap the zip in the Files app) and pick the .ics file here. Re-import any time to refresh. Times assume the events are in your phone’s time zone.
